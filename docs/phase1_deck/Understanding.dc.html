Problem Understanding 04 / 13

How the clinical learning shaped the project

01
Clinical knowledge provided
Hirschberg CLR is the standard first-line estimation test for ocular alignment; reflex decentration converts to an approximate clinical angle.
02
Understanding of the problem
The test itself needs only a light source and an observer — what it actually needs is the trained eye that interprets it. That interpretation is the bottleneck, not the equipment.
03
The specific problem we address
Automate the interpretation step, so a non-specialist can obtain the same objective measurement and a defensible referral decision.
04
Our proposed solution
A phone camera and its torch reproduce the reflex; computer vision measures the bilateral asymmetry and returns a triage tier with a clinical angle.
